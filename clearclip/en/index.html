ClearClip for Android

Clean up the parts you choose.

Select areas and time ranges in a video, then blur them or fill them with surrounding frames using AI restoration. Your original stays untouched; the result is saved as a new file.

Read the privacy policyContact support

How it works

Choose areas and timing

Move and resize regions, add more than one, and choose when each region applies.

Blur or restore

Use blur to cover a region or AI restoration to blend it with nearby video frames.

Preview and save

Review the result and save it as a new video. The original file is not overwritten.

Designed for your device

On-device video processing

Selected videos and region settings are processed on your device, not uploaded to the app operator’s server.

Continue recent work

Open recent editing records saved on your device and continue from where you left off.

Four interface languages

Choose Korean, English, Japanese, or Spanish in the app.
